# Salt Lake City Population Health Research: Health Vulnerabilities and Disparities

## Executive Summary

This comprehensive epidemiological research investigates Salt Lake City's health vulnerabilities and disparities, with focus on ZIP codes 84101-84129. The analysis examines leading causes of death, chronic disease prevalence, mental health indicators, substance abuse rates, maternal and infant health outcomes, and geographic health disparities between east and west Salt Lake City.

From the County Health Rankings & Roadmaps portal, the following data infrastructure was confirmed:
- **Interactive Health Snapshot Available**: Salt Lake County data accessible through county-snapshot visualization tool
- **Health Outcome Measures**: Mortality, morbidity, and quality of life indicators
- **Health Factor Measures**: Health behaviors, clinical care, social & economic factors, physical environment
- **Ranking System**: Comparative rankings within Utah state counties

### 2.2 CDC WONDER Database Access Confirmed
Successfully accessed CDC's Multiple Cause of Death database containing:
- **Mortality Data Coverage**: 1999-2020 for all U.S. counties including Salt Lake County
- **Demographic Stratification**: By age, race/ethnicity, sex, and geographic location
- **Cause of Death Classifications**: ICD-10 codes, 113 selected causes, injury categories
- **Data Use Restrictions**: Counts ≤9 suppressed for confidentiality
- **Rate Calculations**: Crude death rates, age-adjusted death rates with 95% confidence intervals

### 2.3 Literature Search Infrastructure (PubMed)
Confirmed access to comprehensive biomedical literature database:
- **Database Size**: 39+ million citations from MEDLINE and life science journals
- **Search Capabilities**: Advanced search options for geographic and health topic filtering
- **Full-Text Access**: Links to PubMed Central and publisher websites when available

## 3. Health Vulnerabilities Assessment Framework

### 3.1 Leading Causes of Death (Preliminary Framework)
**Expected Priority Areas Based on National and Utah State Data:**
1. **Cardiovascular Disease**
   - Heart disease and stroke
   - Hypertension-related mortality

2. **Cancer**
   - Lung, breast, colorectal, prostate cancers
   - Environmental exposure-related malignancies

3. **Unintentional Injuries**
   - Motor vehicle accidents
   - Falls and poisonings
   - Drug overdose deaths

4. **Chronic Lower Respiratory Disease**
   - COPD and asthma exacerbations
   - Air quality-related respiratory conditions

5. **Suicide and Mental Health-Related Deaths**
   - Particularly relevant given Utah's unique demographic patterns

### 3.2 Chronic Disease Prevalence Framework
**Target Conditions for Detailed Analysis:**

**Diabetes:**

- Diabetic complications and mortality
- Access to diabetes care and management

**Cardiovascular Disease:**

- Confidentiality requirements for geographic analysis
